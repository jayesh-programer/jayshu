Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential order fulfillment details including your name, email address, physical shipping address, phone number, and purchase transaction records. Payment card data is processed through industry-standard tokenized encryption and is never stored on our database servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to process orders, verify payments, generate delivery manifests for shipping carriers, calculate appropriate discounts or taxes, and respond to support inquiries.

3. Data Protection & Security

We deploy standard security controls including HTTPS transport security, server-side parameter verification, Django CSRF countermeasures, and password hashing using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 signatures.

4. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to inspect, update, or request deletion of your account records by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@apexstore.local.
